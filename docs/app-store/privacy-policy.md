# My Car Concierge Driver — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: May 16, 2026_

This Privacy Policy explains how **My Car Concierge, LLC** ("My Car Concierge,"
"we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects information when
you use the **My Car Concierge Driver** mobile application (the "Driver App"),
which is used by drivers contracted to perform vehicle-concierge services for
My Car Concierge.

If you are a passenger or member of My Car Concierge, see the separate
[mycarconcierge.com privacy policy](https://mycarconcierge.com/privacy)
for the consumer side of the service.

## 1. Information we collect

When you use the Driver App we collect the following categories of
information:

- **Account & identity** — your name, phone number, email address, and a
  government-issued ID number used solely to run a background check.
- **Driving credentials** — driver's license image, vehicle insurance
  document, and the data extracted from those documents (license number,
  expiration date, insurance carrier).
- **Banking & payout information** — the last four digits of the bank
  account or debit card you connect through our payments processor for
  receiving ride payouts. Full account numbers are stored only by our
  payments processor, never on our servers.
- **Location data** — your device's precise geographic location. We use
  precise location both while the app is in use (to determine eligibility
  for nearby ride dispatch) and in the background during an active ride
  (so dispatch and the rider can track progress to pickup and drop-off).
- **Ride history & earnings** — every ride request you receive, accept,
  decline, complete, or cancel, along with the associated fare,
  timestamps, pickup / drop-off addresses, and payout amount.
- **Device & push token** — a device identifier and Apple Push
  Notification service (APNs) token so we can send ride-request,
  cancellation, and payout notifications.
- **Support communications** — messages you send to our in-app AI
  assistant or to our human support team.
- **Diagnostic logs** — crash reports and basic performance telemetry.

## 2. How we use information

We use the information above to:

- Verify your identity and run a background check before activating your
  driver account.
- Match you with ride requests based on your location, status, and
  eligibility.
- Power live ride navigation, pickup tracking, and arrival ETAs.
- Process payouts for completed rides through our payments processor.
- Send you transactional notifications (new ride requests, cancellations,
  payout confirmations).
- Respond to your support requests.
- Investigate fraud, safety incidents, or violations of our Driver
  Agreement.
- Comply with legal obligations, including tax reporting on payouts.

We do **not** use your data for advertising or for tracking you across
unrelated apps or websites.

## 3. Third parties we share information with

We share information only with the service providers required to operate
the Driver App, and only the minimum needed for each provider's role:

| Provider | Purpose | Data shared |
|----------|---------|-------------|
| **Supabase, Inc.** | Database, authentication, realtime messaging | Account, ride, location, and document records |
| **Stripe, Inc.** | Payouts and payment processing | Bank/debit account details, payout amounts, tax information |
| **Twilio, Inc.** | SMS verification and ride notifications | Phone number, ride event metadata |
| **Apple Inc. (APNs)** | Push notifications | Device token, notification payload |
| **BackgroundChecks.com** | Background check on new drivers | Name, DOB, license number, government ID |
| **Resend, Inc.** | Transactional email | Email address, message content |

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your data with
data brokers or advertising networks.

We may disclose information when required by law, valid legal process, or
to protect the safety of riders, drivers, or the public.

## 4. Location data — special notice

The Driver App requests **While Using the App** location permission when
you first go online. Once you accept a ride, iOS may automatically prompt
you to upgrade to **Always Allow** so we can continue tracking your
location with the screen locked during the ride. You can revoke either
permission at any time from your iOS Settings → My Car Concierge Driver
→ Location.

Background location is used **only** during an active ride and is stopped
as soon as the ride is completed or cancelled.

## 5. Data retention

- Active driver records are retained as long as your account is active.
- After account deletion (see Section 7), your personal profile is
  anonymized within minutes, but ride records, payout records, and
  audit logs are retained for accounting, tax, and safety purposes for
  up to **seven (7) years** as required by applicable law.
- Background check results are retained for the term of your engagement
  plus the period required by the Fair Credit Reporting Act.

## 6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

- Access the personal information we hold about you.
- Correct inaccurate information.
- Delete your account and personal information (see Section 7).
- Object to certain processing.
- Receive a copy of your data in a portable format.

To exercise any of these rights, email
**privacy@mycarconcierge.com**. We will respond within 30 days.

## 7. Account deletion

You can permanently delete your driver account directly inside the app:
**Settings → Delete Account**. A two-step confirmation is required.

When you delete your account:

- Your authentication credentials are removed and your sessions are
  revoked.
- Your profile (name, email, phone, documents, photo, location, payout
  details) is anonymized in our database.
- Ride history, payout history, and audit logs are retained in
  anonymized form for accounting and safety as described in Section 5.
- The phone number tied to your account is freed so you can re-register
  later if you choose.

Deletion is blocked while you have an active ride, a pending payout in
flight, or an unpaid earned balance, so that no money owed to you is
lost. Resolve those first and try again.

You can also request deletion by emailing
**privacy@mycarconcierge.com**.

## 8. Children

The Driver App is intended only for users aged 21 and over and is not
directed at children.

## 9. Security

We use industry-standard encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest, role-
based access controls, and audit logging for sensitive operations. No
system is perfectly secure; if you believe your account has been
compromised, contact us immediately.

## 10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make
material changes, we will notify you through the app and update the
"Last updated" date above. Continued use of the Driver App after the
effective date constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
