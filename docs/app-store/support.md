## Common questions

### How do I get approved as a driver?
After you sign up with your phone number, complete the application
form (profile, license, and insurance). We run a background check
through our screening partner; approval typically takes 2–3 business
days.

### How do I receive ride requests?
Tap **Go Online** on the Home screen. Once online, eligible ride
requests in your area appear as a full-screen pop-up with the fare,
pickup, drop-off, and a countdown timer. Tap **Accept** to take the
ride or **Decline** to pass.

### How are payouts handled?
Earnings are paid out automatically every week to the bank account
you connect under Settings → Payments. You can also tap **Instant
Pay** in the Earnings screen to cash out your available balance
immediately for a small processing fee.

### How do I update my insurance or license document?
Go to **Settings → Documents** and re-upload. We review updates
within one business day.

### How do I delete my account?
Open **Settings → Delete Account** and follow the two-step
confirmation. Your profile is anonymized immediately; ride history
and payout records are retained as required for accounting.

### My ride request didn't reach me — what happened?
Make sure (1) you are online, (2) location services are enabled
("While Using" or "Always"), and (3) notifications are allowed for
the My Car Concierge Driver app in your iOS Settings. If the issue
persists, contact support with the time and your driver ID.
