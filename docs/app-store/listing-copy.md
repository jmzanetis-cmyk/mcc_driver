## Description

```
My Car Concierge Driver is the official driver portal for My Car Concierge,
the premium vehicle concierge service. Once you've been approved through our
background check, the app puts every ride request, every navigation step, and
every payout right at your fingertips.

WHAT YOU GET

• Real-time ride dispatch — Go online with one tap. When a concierge ride is
  available nearby, you get an instant notification with the fare, pickup
  location, drop-off, and estimated time. Accept, decline, or let the offer
  pass — it's your call.

• Door-to-door navigation — From en route to pickup, to arrival, to ride in
  progress, to complete: the app walks you through every stage and keeps
  dispatch informed so the member knows exactly when you'll arrive.

• Transparent earnings — See your total earnings, today's earnings, and a
  weekly chart. Tap any completed ride to see the breakdown.

• Instant Pay — Don't want to wait for the weekly payout? Cash out your
  available balance in seconds.

• Tandem rides — When the job requires two drivers (such as delivering a
  member's vehicle while a second driver follows behind), the app pairs you
  up and keeps both drivers in sync.

• AI assistant — Stuck on a policy question or wondering how a ride works?
  Ask the in-app assistant any time.

• Built for premium service — Every detail of the app, from the matte black
  navigation screen to the gold-accent ride card, is designed to feel like
  the white-glove concierge experience our members expect.

REQUIREMENTS

• Be at least 21 years old
• Hold a valid U.S. driver's license
• Maintain current automobile insurance
• Pass our background check and motor-vehicle-record review

PRIVACY & SAFETY

We collect only what we need to dispatch rides, navigate, and pay you.
Location is shared only while you're online or on an active ride. You can
permanently delete your account at any time from Settings → Delete Account.

Have questions? Email support@mycarconcierge.com or use the in-app
assistant.

By downloading the app you agree to the My Car Concierge Driver Terms of
Service and Privacy Policy linked from the sign-in screen.
```
